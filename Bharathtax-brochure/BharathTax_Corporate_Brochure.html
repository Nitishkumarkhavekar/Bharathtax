ABOUT WENVIA BHARAT TAX

India's first citation-grounded, sovereign AI assistant for tax research, rulings and drafting — a Wenvia Global product.

Wenvia BharatTax is the tax-research copilot built exclusively for India's income-tax professionals — officers, CIT(A) benches, chartered accountants and legal counsel. It answers tax questions with citations that trace back to the exact section, proviso or CBDT circular they came from — and when the evidence isn't there, it refuses to answer rather than invent one. In a profession where every position must survive an audit or a bench, that refusal is the foundation, not a footnote.

VISION

Make every Indian tax professional twice as effective — with an assistant that is always cited, always trustworthy, and always inside their walls.

MISSION

Build the sovereign AI layer for Indian tax law — grounded in primary sources, refusing to guess, deployable in India, and priced for every wing of every department.

VALUES

Citations before eloquence. Honest refusal over confident invention. India-first design, always.

Generic AI tools were not built for Indian tax law. They hallucinate sections, cite circulars that don't exist, and send sensitive data to foreign clouds. Wenvia BharatTax was built to fix all three — for the professionals who cannot afford to be wrong.
