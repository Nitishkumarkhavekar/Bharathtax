01 · About Us

ABOUT WENVIA BHARAT TAX

India's first citation-grounded, sovereign AI assistant for tax research, rulings and drafting — a Wenvia Global product.

Wenvia BharatTax is the tax-research copilot built exclusively for India's income-tax professionals — officers, CIT(A) benches, chartered accountants, and legal counsel. We do one thing, and we do it precisely: answer tax questions with citations that trace back to the exact section, sub-section, proviso, or CBDT circular they came from.

Every claim Wenvia BharatTax makes is anchored in primary Indian tax law. If the evidence isn't in the corpus, the assistant refuses to answer — plainly and honestly. This isn't a feature. It's the foundation. In a regulated profession where every position must survive an audit or a bench, an AI that confidently invents section numbers is worse than no AI at all.

Why we exist.

Generic AI tools were not built for Indian tax law. They hallucinate sections. They cite circulars that don't exist. They send sensitive client data to foreign clouds. Wenvia BharatTax was built to fix all three, at once, for the professionals who cannot afford to be wrong.

Act · Rules · Circulars

Full Income Tax Act, Rules 1962 and CBDT circulars indexed — searchable to the exact provision.

SC · HC · ITAT

Curated Supreme Court and High Court corpus plus live tribunal rulings on demand.

ITBA-Ready DOCX

Appellate orders, notices and assessment orders drafted straight to signature-ready DOCX.
