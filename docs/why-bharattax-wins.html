"They already pay for Taxmann / ChatGPT" — how to answer

We already subscribe to Taxmann.
Keep it — we're not a replacement. Taxmann tells you what the law says; BharatTax drafts your order, watches your limitation dates, and computes your interest. Different purchase, different job. And for the assessment / recovery / investigation side, Taxmann doesn't serve you at all — we do.
Why not just use ChatGPT?
Because it will confidently cite a section that's been amended or doesn't exist — you can't put that before a bench — and it won't draft your 143(3) order or flag a time-bar. Ours is cited, and honest when it doesn't know.
How do we trust an AI with our case data?
Because your case data stays on your infrastructure. Taxmann never had to solve this — it doesn't hold your case files. ChatGPT can't — it's a foreign cloud. Only we can keep the working data under your control.

Data stays with the government — without a server in every office

Sovereignty doesn't mean a box in every ward. It means the data sits on government-controlled infrastructure and the government controls it — one central instance, exactly like ITBA / Insight / TRACES already run.

  • Managed sovereign instance (default for govt): a dedicated, isolated instance on gov-approved Indian infrastructure — their tenant, their keys — that we operate. On-prem-grade control, SaaS-grade convenience, zero ops burden on any office.
  • Central gov-hosted or air-gapped on-prem: for the highest-control wings, the full stack + a local AI model runs inside the department's own network. Nothing leaves.
  • Officers manage nothing — a browser or a signed desktop app connects to the central instance over the department network.
