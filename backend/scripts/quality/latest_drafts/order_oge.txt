The appeal filed by the assessee, M/s Anvi Textiles LLP, against the assessment order passed under section 143(3) dated 27.03.2024 for Assessment Year 2019-20 has been disposed of by the Commissioner of Income-tax (Appeals) / National Faceless Appeal Centre vide the order cited above.

2. In compliance with the provisions of section 250(2) of the Income-tax Act, 1961, the directions contained in the appellate order are hereby given effect to as follows:

2.1. **Deletion of Addition under Section 68:** The addition of Rs. 62,00,000 made under section 68 of the Act on account of unexplained share capital is DELETED. The appellate authority held the share capital to be genuine on the strength of the PAN, Income Tax Returns, and bank statements of the subscribers.

2.2. **Restriction of Disallowance under Section 40A(3):** The disallowance of Rs. 8,40,000 made under section 40A(3) of the Act is RESTRICTED to Rs. 2,10,000. The appellate authority sustained the disallowance only in respect of payments exceeding Rs. 10,000 made in a single day to a single party otherwise than by way of an account payee cheque or account payee bank draft or use of electronic clearing system through a bank account.
