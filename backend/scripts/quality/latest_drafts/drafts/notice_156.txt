If the amount demanded is not paid within the specified period, you shall be treated as an assessee-in-default under section 220(4) of the Income-tax Act, 1961, and the following consequences shall ensue:

    (ii) A penalty under section 221 may be imposed, which shall not exceed the amount of tax in arrears.

5. **Right of Appeal and Stay of Demand:**

    (ii) You may seek a stay of the demand under section 220(6) pending the disposal of the appeal, provided you pay 20% of the disputed demand amount and make an application to the Assessing Officer.
