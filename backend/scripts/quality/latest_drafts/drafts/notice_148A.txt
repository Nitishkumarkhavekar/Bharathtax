Sub: Show-Cause Notice under section 148A(1) of the Income-tax Act, 1961, for the Assessment Year 2021-22.

1. Information has been received/available on record which suggests that income chargeable to tax has escaped assessment for the Assessment Year 2021-22, within the meaning of sections 148 and 149 of the Income-tax Act, 1961 (the Act).

2. The substance of the information suggesting escapement of income is as follows:
    Information available from the Insight Portal and the Annual Information Statement (AIS) indicates that M/s Silverstone Traders Pvt. Ltd. (the Assessee) made three cash deposits aggregating Rs. 45,00,000 in the current account with HDFC Bank (a/c ******7841) between 12.04.2020 and 28.03.2021. The return of income filed by the Assessee on 30.11.2021 declared a total income of Rs. 8,20,140. These cash deposits of Rs. 45,00,000 are not reflected in the said return of income, and no exemption or exclusion claimed in the return would cover these deposits.

3. Based on the above information, it is suggested that income amounting to Rs. 45,00,000, being unexplained cash credits or unexplained money, has escaped assessment, which is likely to be chargeable to tax under section 68 or section 69A of the Act.

4. You are hereby called upon to show cause on or before 18.09.2026 as to why a notice under section 148 of the Act should not be issued in your case for the Assessment Year 2021-22.

5. Your reply, along with all supporting documents and evidence, must be furnished electronically through the designated portal within the stipulated time.

6. Please note that if no response is received by the specified date (18.09.2026), it shall be presumed that you have nothing to offer in this matter, and an order under section 148A(3) of the Act will be passed on the basis of the material available on record, deciding whether it is a fit case for the issue of a notice under section 148 of the Act within the limitation prescribed by section 149 of the Act.
